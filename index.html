WELCOME

A LOCAL TAKEAWAY WITH A BIG HEART

Amina's Fast Food was founded in 2021 in Port Elizabeth. It started as a small family takeaway stall and has grown while continuing to provide affordable meals and friendly service to the local community.

Our aim is to make everyday meals convenient and enjoyable. From burgers and crispy chips to kota and fat cakes, our menu includes familiar favourites for different tastes and appetites.
