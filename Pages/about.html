ABOUT AMINA'S FAST FOOD

Learn more about our story, mission and community.

Our Story

Amina's Fast Food was founded in 2021 in Port Elizabeth by Amina Langa, with support from Nouthando Xayimpi. The business began as a small family takeaway stall serving quick, affordable and flavourful meals.

Over time, the business has grown while keeping its focus on friendly service, accessible prices and food that brings people together.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide delicious, affordable and fast meals while creating a welcoming experience for every customer. We also aim to support local families and create employment opportunities in the surrounding community.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become one of Port Elizabeth's most loved local fast food brands, known for affordable meals, reliable service and positive community involvement.

Our Values

Quality: We care about the meals we serve.

Customer service: We treat customers with respect.

Affordability: We offer choices for different budgets.

Community: We believe in supporting local people.
