Our Story

Amina's Fast Food was founded in 2021 in Port Elizabeth by Amina Langa, with support from Nouthando Xayimpi.


The business started as a small family takeaway stall serving quick, affordable flavourful meals. Over the years,Amina's Fast Food has grown and become known in years,for affordable prices,friendly services and supporting employment in the local community.

Our Mission

Our mission is provide delicious, affordable and fast meals that brings people together while supporting the local community. We are committed to creating job opportunities and employing people surrounding the areas.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become Port Elizabeth's most loved local fast food brand for affordable meals and great service,while continuning to empower local people.

Our community

Amina's Fast food believes in supporting local families and building a stronger local economy through employment and affordable food.
