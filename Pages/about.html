OUR STORY

From a Small Takeaway to a Local Food Business

Amina's Fast Food was founded in 2021 in Port Elizabeth by Amina Langa, with support from Nouthando Xayimpi. The business started as a small family takeaway stall with a simple idea: provide tasty food that customers can enjoy without spending too much.

From the beginning, the focus has been on fresh meals, friendly service and affordable choices. Burgers, chips, kota meals, fat cakes and combo deals give customers a range of familiar fast-food options for different occasions.

As the business grows, Amina's Fast Food aims to remain connected to the local community while improving the customer experience and maintaining the values that shaped the business from the start.

OUR MISSION

What We Aim to Do

Our mission is to provide delicious, affordable and convenient meals while creating a welcoming experience for every customer. We aim to serve customers with care, maintain consistent service and make everyday meals accessible to the surrounding community.

OUR VISION

Where We Are Going

Our vision is to grow into a recognised local fast-food brand in Port Elizabeth, known for affordable meals, reliable service, friendly customer care and positive involvement in the community.

OUR COMMUNITY

Serving Local Customers

Amina's Fast Food is based in KwaZakele, Port Elizabeth, Eastern Cape. The business is built around serving local customers who want convenient meals at accessible prices.

We believe a local food business is also a place where people can connect, enjoy a meal and support businesses within their community.
